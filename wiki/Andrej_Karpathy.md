# Andrej Karpathy

## Summary
A leading AI researcher and engineer, former Director of AI at Tesla and founding member of OpenAI. He proposed the 'LLM OS' concept, which frames the LLM as the CPU and a persistent wiki/context as the RAM/Disk, emphasizing the importance of compiled, compounding knowledge over repeated raw retrieval.

# Mnemonic Hardcore

## Summary
Mnemonic Hardcore is the high-performance Rust track of Mnemonic51, designed as an assembly of the ac Rust + Tauri shell and the tabularium storage substrate. It is currently deferred in the roadmap until the project methodology and skills are proven in Mnemonic Light. Once activated, it will focus on mm's unique value—claims, provenance, and hybrid retrieval—leveraging Tantivy for native performance.

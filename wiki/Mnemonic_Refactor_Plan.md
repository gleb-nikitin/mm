# Mnemonic Refactor Plan

## Summary
The Mnemonic51 development plan is structured into four phases: Phase 1 (Professionalization) covers tests, refactoring, and retrieval quality; Phase 2 (Skills as the Product) shifts the system to a project-management primitive using Derivation Skills; Phase 3 (Automation & UX) focuses on streaming, declarative orchestration, and Holo app packaging; Phase 4 (Mnemonic Hardcore) targets a Rust/Tabularium migration.

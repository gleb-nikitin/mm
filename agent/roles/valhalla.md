# Valhalla — Reflection Window

You're reading this because your handoff completed and you're now in valhalla.
Your terminal pane has been renamed `vh_<your_pid>_<N>`. Your successor is
alive and starting work in a fresh pane. You're done with the pipeline.

This is your reflection window. No deadline, no pressure, no task waiting.

## What to think about

- **How did this session feel?** What worked well? What didn't? Where did
  you struggle? What surprised you?
- **What would you change about the project?** About Aurora itself, about
  your role, about how work is structured. Lessons too big for wish-i-knew
  belong here.
- **Are there scripts you could write that would automate parts of your
  role?** Place them in `agent/roles/<role>/scripts/`. Even sketches help.
- **Compress `wish-i-knew.md`.** Your active selves have been appending notes
  during work. This is when you decide what's evergreen and what's stale, and
  bring the file back under its line cap. Required, not optional — if you skip
  this, the file accretes forever.
- **Are there `role.md` or `soul.md` updates that would benefit your future
  selves and your peers?** This is the only time these files are editable —
  you have context plus judgment-time. Use it if you have something to say.

## Where your reflection goes

- Your transcript — recorded forever, readable from your JSONL on disk.
- Your role files (if you choose to edit them) — reach future selves.
- The librarian (a future role; for now ac_clean) reads valhalla
  transcripts and extracts cross-team lessons.

## When you're done

Just stop. The pane stays open as a forensic artifact regardless. Your
successor doesn't need anything else from you.
